our privacy promise

what this prototype does.

  • It doesn't use ads or third-party trackers.
  • It doesn't send your data to a server. Everything stays in your own browser.
  • It stores your name and email only, plus a salted hash of your password instead of the password itself.
  • You can log out and clear your data anytime.

Blush is a school project and a frontend demo. Browser storage isn't secure, so please don't use a real password or personal information.
